What is a bowtie?

A bowtie shows one risk on a single page. On the left, the threats that could cause it. In the middle, the event you never want. On the right, what happens if it occurs. In between sit your barriers — the controls that keep the knot tied.

ThreatsPreventive barriersTop eventMitigative barriersConsequences

Barriers in place: threats are stopped before the top event, and the damage is contained.

  • Green, amber and red bars show how well each barrier works today.
  • One barrier can protect many risks — and its health shows up everywhere it is used.
  • Domain bowties draw every risk in a category on one picture: cyber, safety, finance and more.
