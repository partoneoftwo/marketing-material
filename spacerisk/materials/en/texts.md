## Short description
SpaceRisk is a friendly ISO 31000 risk register where teams keep their risks and barriers up to date every month, and leaders see the whole picture in bowties, heat maps and a financial view.

## Medium description
SpaceRisk helps organisations manage risk where it lives: in the teams. Every month, team members review their risks and check the barriers that protect against them; team leads and project managers submit a short return; and the CEO gets an executive summary with heat maps, bowties and a financial perspective — Value at Risk, expected loss and the return on controls. Everything exports to PDF, PowerPoint and Excel.

## Long description
Most risk registers die in a spreadsheet. SpaceRisk keeps yours alive by making risk part of every team's month. Each risk is drawn as a bowtie: the threats that could cause it, the event you want to avoid, its consequences, and the barriers in between — coloured by how well they work today. Teams review their risks and check their barriers in a monthly cycle; team leads and project managers attest the result; and executives see everything on one dashboard, from a heat map to a financial view that puts a value on risk and on the controls that reduce it. A barrier panel shows which controls carry the most weight and where the gaps are. Domain bowties draw every risk in a category on one page. Hazards, consequences, projects and executive summaries export to PDF, PowerPoint and Excel in one click. SpaceRisk can be hosted by us or self-hosted, follows ISO 31000 and comes with 92 realistic showcase companies, from hospitals and airlines to oil platforms and hedge funds.

## Feature blurbs
- **ISO 31000 risk register:** Likelihood × consequence, inherent, residual and target ratings, appetite and treatment plans.
- **Monthly review cycle:** Every team reviews every risk and checks every barrier it owns, then the lead submits the return.
- **Bowties and barrier panel:** Per-risk and per-domain bowties, a barrier board and a risk × barrier matrix that reveals gaps.
- **Hazards and consequences:** See what you are really protecting against, grouped by domain, across all teams.
- **Financial perspective:** Value at Risk, expected annual loss before and after barriers, and the return on every krone spent on controls.
- **Executive summary:** Key messages written from the data, plus your own comment — ready for the board.
- **One-click exports:** PDF, PowerPoint and Excel for every page, every team and every project.
- **92 sector showcases:** Start with a realistic demo company from your own industry, from hospital to oil platform.
