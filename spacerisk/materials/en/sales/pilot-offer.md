# SpaceRisk 90-day pilot

**Draft offer. Terms are confirmed in a written agreement before the pilot starts.**

## What you get
Run your real risk work in SpaceRisk for 90 days, with hands-on help from the person who builds it.

- **Risk management (ISO 31000):** risk register, bowties and barriers, monthly team reviews, dashboards and an
  executive summary in PDF and PowerPoint.
- **ISO 27001 module:** clauses, Statement of Applicability, readiness, the ISMS registers, plus the cyber programme
  and technical assessments.
- **Compliance module:** NIS2, DORA, GDPR, ISO 22301, 45001, 27701, 42001, CRA and the Norwegian acts
  (digitalsikkerhetsloven, DORA-loven), with their registers and exports.
- **Hosting your way:** hosted by us, or on your own Cloudron server. Your data is always yours: a full
  export is one click away, at any time.
- **Onboarding:** a 2-hour setup workshop, import of your existing register (Excel), and a fortnightly 30-minute
  check-in.

## What we ask of you
- A named pilot owner, and 2–5 teams who use it in at least two monthly review cycles.
- Honest feedback: a short call every two weeks and a final review at day 90.
- If you're happy at the end: permission to name you as a reference, or a short quote (anonymous if you prefer).

## Success criteria (agreed at the start)
Pick two or three, for example:
- The risk register and controls have moved out of spreadsheets.
- Monthly team reviews are done in SpaceRisk, on time.
- The board or CEO report takes under one hour to prepare.
- A gap list exists for your next ISO 27001 audit or NIS2 / DORA review.
