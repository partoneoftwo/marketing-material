# Pilot runbook

**Draft, for our own use.** How to run the 90-day pilot described in [`pilot-offer.md`](pilot-offer.md), from first
yes to the decision at day 90.

## Before the start
- [ ] Pilot agreement signed ([`pilot-agreement.md`](pilot-agreement.md)), with two or three success criteria written in.
- [ ] Hosting chosen: their own Cloudron server, or hosted by us.
- [ ] If hosted by us: data processing agreement signed, hosting country stated, backup tested (restore, not only dump).
- [ ] Named pilot owner, and the 2–5 teams with a lead for each.
- [ ] Their current register received (Excel), and the language chosen.
- [ ] Dates in both calendars: workshop, fortnightly check-ins, day-90 review.

## Week 0: setup workshop (2 hours)
| Minutes | |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | Their goals, and the success criteria read aloud |
| 15–45 | Company, teams and users set up together; risk scales and appetite agreed |
| 45–80 | Import of the register; the pilot owner corrects five risks live |
| 80–105 | First monthly cycle opened; a team lead walks through one review |
| 105–120 | Which module comes second (ISO 27001 or a compliance framework), and homework before check-in 1 |

Afterwards, the same day: send login details through a safe channel, and a one-page note of what was agreed.

## Fortnightly check-in (30 minutes)
1. What did you do in SpaceRisk since last time? (Look at it together, don't ask for a description.)
2. Where did you get stuck or go back to the spreadsheet?
3. One thing to fix before next time, on each side.
4. Status against the success criteria.

Keep a running list of every request, with who asked and why. Fix small things within the week and say so.

## Warning signs
| Sign | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nobody has logged in for two weeks | Call the pilot owner; offer to sit with one team for a review |
| Only the pilot owner uses it | Ask to join a team meeting and run the monthly review there |
| The spreadsheet is still the master copy | Find the one thing the spreadsheet does that SpaceRisk doesn't |
| Check-ins get cancelled | Ask plainly whether the pilot still has a sponsor |

## Day 60: prepare the decision
- [ ] Ask who decides on continuing, and when their budget round is.
- [ ] Send the price and plan that fit their size; the pilot fee is credited against the first year.
- [ ] Ask what must be true at day 90 for a yes.

## Day 90: final review (45 minutes)
1. Go through the success criteria one by one: met, partly met, not met.
2. What they would miss if it was switched off tomorrow.
3. What is missing for them to continue.
4. The decision, or the date of the decision.
5. If yes: reference or quote, as agreed. If no: a full export handed over, and their data deleted on the agreed date.

## After
- [ ] Write down what was learned: what worked, what didn't, what was asked for.
- [ ] Update the offer, the price and this runbook.
