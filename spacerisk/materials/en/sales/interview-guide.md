# SpaceRisk discovery interview guide

**Purpose:** learn how risk, ISO 27001 and compliance work is done today, what it costs and what hurts. Don't sell in
these conversations. Aim for 10 interviews before building more features.

**Who to talk to:** CISOs, risk managers, compliance and DPO leads, and IT managers in mid-size organisations (50–2,000
staff). Start with sectors that have new legal duties: Norwegian banks and insurers (DORA-loven), and hospitals,
energy and digital service providers (digitalsikkerhetsloven, NIS2).

**Format:** 30–40 minutes, video or in person. Ask for permission to take notes. Don't record without consent, and
don't store anything confidential they share.

---

## 1. Opening (2 min)
> "Thanks for your time. I'm building a tool for risk management and compliance, and I'd like to learn how you work
> today. I won't try to sell you anything. There are no wrong answers, and complaints are the most useful part."

## 2. Their role and context (5 min)
1. What is your role, and who do you report to?
2. How many people work on risk, security or compliance? Full-time or alongside other work?
3. Which standards and laws apply to you? (ISO 27001, ISO 31000, NIS2, DORA, GDPR, ISO 22301, others)
4. Are you certified, or working towards certification? When is the next audit?

## 3. How they work today (10 min)
5. Walk me through the last time you updated your risk register. What did you open first?
6. Where do risks, controls, incidents and suppliers live today? (Excel, SharePoint, a GRC tool, a consultant)
7. Who else contributes? How do you collect input from teams or departments?
8. How do you report to the CEO or board? How long does it take to prepare?
9. What happened in your last audit or supervisory review? What did you have to scramble for?

## 4. Pain and cost (10 min)
10. What is the most frustrating part of this work today?
11. If you could fix one thing tomorrow, what would it be?
12. Roughly how many hours a month go into keeping the register and reports up to date?
13. Have you tried a dedicated tool? What happened? Why did you keep it, or drop it?
14. What do you pay today for tools or consultants in this area? (A range is fine.)
15. When a new law arrives (NIS2, DORA), how do you work out what it means for you?

## 5. Buying (5 min)
16. If you bought a tool for this, who would decide, and who would need to approve?
17. What would a vendor have to show before you could buy? (Single sign-on, two-factor login, data location,
    certifications, references, contract terms, an exit plan for your data)
18. Cloud or self-hosted: does it matter? Why?
19. What budget would this come from, and when is it set?

## 6. Reaction (only if time allows, 5 min)
Show one screen only, such as a bowtie or the DORA page, not a demo.
20. What is your first reaction? What is missing?
21. Would this replace something you use today, or come on top?
22. Would you take part in a 90-day pilot? (If yes: [pilot-offer.md](pilot-offer.md))

## 7. Close (2 min)
23. Who else should I talk to?
24. May I follow up in a few weeks?

**Signals worth acting on:** the same pain from three or more people; someone who already pays for a workaround; a
deadline (audit, supervisor, new law); a direct "can I try it?".

**Signals to be careful with:** "nice tool" with no pain behind it; interest from people who can't buy; requests
for a feature only one person mentions.
