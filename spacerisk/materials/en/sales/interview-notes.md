## What to do with the result

After ten interviews:

| What you saw | What it means | Next |
|---|---|---|
| 3 or more asked for price or a pilot | There is demand in this group | Offer the pilot ([`pilot-offer.md`](pilot-offer.md)) to those people first |
| Interest, but the same blocker from 3 or more | The blocker is the next thing to build | Plan that feature, then go back to them |
| Problems described, but nobody pays for it today | The pain is real but not budgeted | Try another role (the one holding the budget) or another sector |
| Few describe the problem at all | Wrong group | Change sector or company size before building anything more |

Write the summary you promised the people you interviewed: one page, no names, the five most common findings.
